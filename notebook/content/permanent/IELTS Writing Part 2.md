- Advancements in technology have led to increased integration of AI in vehicles. This progress results from the combined improvements in hardware and AI models. First of all, sensitive and precise detectors deliver environmental information to the control system with high speed and accuracy. Second, excellent chips facilitate AI models in performing extensive calculations, enabling valid responses within a minimal time. Last but not least, a well-trained AI model provides optimal, reliable decisions and strategies. NVIDIA's Orin X chip is an example of hardware enhancement. It is a high-performance, highly integrated AI chip specifically designed for autonomous driving and advanced driving assistance systems. The chip utilizes a 7-nanometer process, a technique that maximizes transistor density on the chip's surface, and boasts outstanding computing power and efficiency.

- Integrating AI in automobiles simplifies driving tasks and should certainly be seen as a positive revolution. On the one hand, AI assists in accident prevention, making distraction and exhaustion while driving less dangerous. On the other hand, AI can undertake repetitive, aggravating tasks, allowing drivers to have a more pleasant experience. For instance, drivers are liberated during a traffic jam, as AI can take over control of the car and navigate through the congestion.
