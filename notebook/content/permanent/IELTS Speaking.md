### hometown
Where is your hometown:
Actually i'm from a small in the south of China. It's called Dongguan, and it's not far from Hong Kong, you might have heard of Hong Kong. Dongguan is next to the sea and havs a warm and rainy climate.

What was it like growing up there: 
To be honest growing up there was pretty good. Dongguan is a well-developed city. There were good schools and there were plenty of leisure activities that young people can do. My family would spend weekends go to the shopping centers, the cinemas, and sometimes go fishing or go to the mountains for a walk.

Has it changed much since you were a child: 
It had changed quite a lot. There had been new business centers built up. The old houses had a renovation as well. And these years you might see some banners along the main roads that written "welcome to Dongguan".
